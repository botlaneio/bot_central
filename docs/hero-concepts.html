Three hero directions for botLane

Rough previews to pick a direction, not finished designs. Each one plays on load and loops. All numbers and jobs are demo data. Inside every animation the accents are black; the violet silk gradient stays on the headline and the main button. The chosen one gets rebuilt properly in React with Motion and the real animated orb (its moods: thinking, working, happy and so on).
